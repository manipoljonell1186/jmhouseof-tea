Classic Milk Tea Recipe

Ingredients (1 serving – 16 oz cup):

  • 1 black tea bag (or 2 tsp loose black tea)

  • 1 cup hot water

  • 2 tbsp brown sugar (adjust to taste)

  • ¼ cup evaporated milk or fresh milk

  • 1 cup ice

For Tapioca Pearls (Pearls):

  • ¼ cup cooked tapioca pearls

  • 1 tbsp brown sugar syrup

Procedure

    Step 1: Brew the Tea

  1. Boil 1 cup of water.

  2. Steep the black tea for 5–7 minutes.

  3. Remove the tea bag and let it cool.

    Step 2: Prepare the Pearls

  1. Cook tapioca pearls according to package instructions.

  2. Drain and mix with brown sugar syrup.

  3. Set aside.

Step 3: Mix the Milk Tea

1.In a shaker or large glass, combine:

  • Brewed tea

  • Brown sugar

  • Milk

Step 4: Assemble

  1. Place tapioca pearls in the bottom of a 16 oz cup.

  2. Fill the cup with ice.

  3. Pour the milk tea mixture over the ice.

  4. Serve with a large milk tea straw.
