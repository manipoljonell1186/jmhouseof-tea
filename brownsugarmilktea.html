Brown Sugar Milk Tea Recipe

Ingredients

(1 Serving)

  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 cup fresh milk (or evaporated milk)
  • 1 tea bag black tea (or 1 tablespoon loose black tea)
  • ½ cup tapioca pearls (cooked)
  • Ice cubes

For the Tapioca Pearls

  1. Boil 4 cups of water.
  2. Add ½ cup tapioca pearls.
  3. Cook for 20–30 minutes or according to package instructions.
  4. Turn off heat and let sit for 15 minutes.
  5. Drain and rinse with warm water.
  6. Prepare the Brown Sugar Syrup
  7. In a small saucepan, combine:
  8. 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  9. 2 tablespoons water

Procedure

Simmer over low heat until thick and syrupy (about 3–5 minutes). Add the cooked tapioca pearls and stir until coated. Brew the Tea Steep the black tea in ½ cup hot water for 3–5 minutes. Remove the tea bag and let the tea cool. Assemble the Drink Spoon the brown sugar pearls and syrup into a tall glass. Swirl the glass so the syrup coats the sides. Add ice cubes. Pour in the fresh milk. Slowly add the brewed tea. Stir before drinking and enjoy!
